A 12-month plan

Work backwards from your intake. Deadlines differ by programme, so treat these as phases, not dates.

12 to 10 months beforeShortlist 6 to 8 programmes. Book your English test and check if GRE is expected.
10 to 8 monthsRead papers from target labs. Email 3 to 5 professors with a specific, short research pitch.
8 to 6 monthsDraft your SOP and CV. Request recommendation letters and give referees your draft SOP.
6 to 4 monthsSubmit applications and separate scholarship forms. Check each portal for its own deadline.
4 to 2 monthsRespond to interviews, accept an offer, and start visa and proof-of-funds paperwork.
Final 2 monthsArrange housing, travel, insurance, and keep certified copies of every document.

FAQ

Are these scores a chance of admission?

No. They only compare your profile against typical expectations. Admission depends on competition, fit and documents.

Why are amounts and deadlines missing?

They change every year and by programme. The official page is the only source worth trusting.

Do I need GRE?

Often optional for Germany and Canada, sometimes recommended for competitive research tracks. Check each programme.

Is my data stored?

No account and no storage. If the API is reachable, your profile is sent only to compute the match.

What if the API is offline?

The page falls back to built-in scoring in your browser, so results still work.

Scholar Compass is a planning aid, not legal or admissions advice. Verify eligibility, funding and deadlines on official pages. Funding is never guaranteed.
